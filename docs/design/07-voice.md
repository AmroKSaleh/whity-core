# Voice and content

Whity sounds like a senior engineer explaining a system to a business owner: plain, exact, calm, and on the owner's side. Whity's own brand speaks English; the product is written in English and Arabic.

## English

- **Plain and direct.** Write to "you", use active verbs, and say what happens. "We" only on the website and in docs, never in the product.
- **Buttons name the action**: "Issue invoice", "Approve", "Revoke credential", "Undo". Never "Submit", "OK", "Confirm" or "Proceed".
- **Short and specific.** A fact beats a promise: "Last used today, 14:02", "12 overdue · JOD 4,860".
- **Sentence case** everywhere. No exclamation marks. No emoji in product or partner material.
- **Numbers**: Western digits, JOD with 3 decimals (JOD 301.600), dates as 27 Oct 2026 (27/10/2026 where space is short), 24-hour times.

## Arabic interface

Simple Modern Standard Arabic in short sentences, written and reviewed by a native speaker before it ships; never unreviewed machine translation. The Arabic in the templates is working copy for that review.

## Agents

- **Describe the action, not the agent**: the agent named by role, then who it acts for, then what it did or proposes. "Billing assistant for Rana · Sent 12 payment reminders."
- **Names by role**: Billing assistant, HR assistant, Front desk. No human first names, no "Whity AI".
- **No "I", no feelings, no apologies.** A proposal title is an imperative with a count: "Send payment reminders to 12 customers".
- **Documents an agent sent say so** in their footer, with the person it acted for.

## Errors

Say what happened and what to do. No apology, no blame, one next step. Technical detail and a copyable reference go behind "Details".

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| Error 403 | You don't have permission to edit roles. Ask an owner. |
| Something went wrong. Please try again later. | Invoices didn't load: the connection timed out. Try again. |
| Success! Your changes have been saved. | Branding saved. Your team sees it on their next page load. |
| Our AI agent is working its magic… | Billing assistant is reading 48 open invoices. |
| Are you sure? | Delete Example Bakery? This deletes the workspace for everyone in it. |

## Words

| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| agent, assistant (named by role) | AI employee, copilot, bot, magic |
| approve, ask first, allowed, scope | autonomous, hands-free, set and forget |
| platform, workspace, app | suite, solution, ecosystem, synergy |
| a button that names the action, such as "Issue invoice" | Submit, OK, Confirm, Proceed |
| Built on Whity | Powered by AI, AI-first |
| your brand, your system (to end users) | white-label (fine with partners) |

## The name

"Whity", one word, capital W, in Latin letters in every language. Products are Whity plus one noun: Whity Core, Whity SDK, Whity Render, Whity ID, Whity Front Office. Third-party plugins name the job first: "Invoicing for Whity". In mockups, use clearly fictional names (Example Bakery) or placeholders ([Tenant name], [price]).
