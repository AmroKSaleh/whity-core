# Delivery note

Travels with the goods and comes back signed. It lists what was ordered and what was delivered, with a notes column for anything short or damaged, and no prices (those are on the invoice). The signature row records who delivered and who received.

Here the delivery for QUO-0318: one large tray arrived damaged, which is why credit note CN-0012 exists. Built from `Doc`, `DocNote`, `DocSign`.
