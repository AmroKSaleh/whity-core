# Tax invoice

An A4 tax invoice rendered by Whity Render in the tenant's brand, English leading with Arabic labels. Built from `Doc` with the stage 6 rules: 16 mm margins, the tenant's logo and details at the start and the title block at the end, the tenant colour on the title only, a rules-only line table, totals 46% wide with the amount in words in both languages, the JoFotara stamp, reference and page at the start of the foot and the QR at the end.

Same document with Arabic leading: Tax invoice (Arabic). Long version with continuation pages: Tax invoice (2 pages).

The QR and reference come from JoFotara when the invoice is issued; the QR here is an example that encodes "EXAMPLE ONLY". Fields, the 16% rate and the "on account" type are illustrative: confirm the required invoice fields with the tenant's accountant and the JoFotara specification before shipping. Bracketed values come from tenant settings.
