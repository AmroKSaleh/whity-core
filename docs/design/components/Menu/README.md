# Menu and Popover

`Menu` lists actions on a thing: an icon, the label, an optional hint and shortcut, dividers, danger actions in the error colour at the end, and disabled items that say why. Arrow keys move, Home and End jump, Enter runs, Escape closes. `Popover` holds a small form next to its button (filters, quick edits) with its own Apply. Both share the floating surface: popover colour, 1px border, 10px corners and the soft shadow (`shadow-float`); items are 32px with 8px corners (¼ of the height).
