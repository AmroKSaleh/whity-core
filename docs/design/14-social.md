# Social and presentation

Every post, slide and cover is one white sheet on the grey ground with the plane under the one thing that matters. One blue element per piece.

## Sizes

| Format | Size | Safe area |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Link preview (Open Graph) | 1200 × 630 | 64 px |
| Square post | 1080 × 1080 | 80 px |
| Portrait post | 1080 × 1350 | 80 px |
| Story | 1080 × 1920 | 250 px top and bottom |
| Avatar | 1000 × 1000 (shown round) | the app icon tile; mark inside the inner 61% |
| Slide | 1920 × 1080 | 96 px |

## Rules

- **Headlines in Readex Pro 600** (decision 3.1), sentence case, three lines at most, on the brand scale (49 · 39 · 31 · 25 · 20) or larger multiples for slides. Body text in Noto Sans; an eyebrow in Geist Mono uppercase, tracked +6%.
- The horizontal lockup at the bottom start, at least 20px tall; the mark alone on avatars (the app icon tile).
- One blue element: the plane, a blueprint panel or the lockup. Never a blue headline on a blue panel.
- Technical posts may use the blueprint pattern for a diagram; product posts show a real screen or document on a sheet.
- No stock photos of robots, servers or handshakes. No emoji. Real names only with permission; otherwise Example Bakery and friends.
- Slides: 16:9, one idea per slide, the same sheet-on-paper layout; decks are built from the Slides tools with these tokens.
- Files: `og-image-1200x630.png` and `avatar-1000.png` in Social.
