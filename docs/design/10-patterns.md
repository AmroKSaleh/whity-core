# Patterns

Two patterns, both from the mark, and one meaning for the dashed line.

## Sheets on paper (everyday)

White sheets with soft corners on the grey ground, one of them on its plane. Use it for social posts, slides, document covers and website sections: one sheet carries the message; the plane sits under the one thing that matters. Headlines in Readex Pro 600, body in Noto Sans. See Social and presentation.

## Blueprint (how it works)

A Whity Blue ground (`blueprint`) with a measured grid of white lines (16px fine, 80px major: `blueprint-line`, `blueprint-line-strong`), drawn boxes in 1.5px white lines and mono labels like a technical drawing ("SHEET 01 · …"). It does not invert with the theme.

- **Use** for architecture, tenancy, the plugin boundary, the MCP and agent surface, request flows: in docs, the developer site and the technical part of a pitch.
- **Never** for pricing, policy, marketing claims or a tenant's own pages.
- Component: `Blueprint` (label, title, children); Tailwind utility `bg-blueprint-grid`.

## Dashed means "not yet"

In both patterns and in the product, a dashed line is something that has not happened: an agent proposal waiting for a person, an optional step, an undone action, an empty slot. Approving sets it down: solid, on the plane.
