# Contributor Licence Agreement

**Whity Core — Individual and Entity CLA, version 1.0**

Before your first contribution is merged, you sign this once. It takes a minute
and it never expires.

---

## Why this exists, plainly

Whity Core is AGPL-3.0-only, and it is also a product. Paid first-party
applications are built on it, and a company that cannot use the AGPL may buy a
commercial licence instead. Both are only possible if one party can license the
whole codebase under terms other than the AGPL.

Copyright does not work like a pool. If ten people contribute and each keeps
their own copyright, then relicensing any part of the project needs **all ten to
agree** — and one person who has changed jobs, changed email address, or simply
says no is enough to block it permanently. Projects have been frozen for years
by exactly this.

So this agreement asks you for **permission**, not for ownership:

- **You keep your copyright.** You can use your own contribution anywhere else,
  for anything, forever. Nothing here takes that away.
- **You grant a licence to relicense.** The project maintainer may distribute
  your contribution under the AGPL *and* under other terms, including
  commercial ones.
- **You confirm you had the right to send it.** That it is your work, and that
  your employer does not own it.

If you would rather not sign, that is a legitimate choice — open an issue and
describe the change instead. Ideas are not copyrightable, and a good bug report
is often worth more than a patch.

## How to sign

Add a line to [`CONTRIBUTORS.md`](CONTRIBUTORS.md) in your first pull request:

```
- Full Name <email@example.com> — CLA v1.0 signed YYYY-MM-DD
```

Committing that line with your pull request is your signature. Contributing on
behalf of a company? Add the company name and confirm in the pull request that
you are authorised to bind it.

---

## For the maintainer

Nothing merges from an outside contributor until their line is in
`CONTRIBUTORS.md`. There is no way to fix this after the fact except by asking
every past contributor individually, and a single refusal is permanent — which
is the entire reason this file exists before it is needed rather than after.
