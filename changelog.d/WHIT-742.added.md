- **Template slots.** A plugin can name the places it needs a document template, such as its order confirmation, and each workspace chooses which of its published templates fills each one. Workspace plugins declare them under `documents.slots` in `plugin.json` (Plugin SDK 1.55). `GET /api/document-template-slots` lists the slots of the workspace's switched-on plugins and what is bound to each, and `PUT /api/document-template-slots/{key}` binds or unbinds a template (`documents:publish`, If-Match required). A template must be published, shared with the workspace, of a kind the slot takes and for its record type. `POST /api/documents` takes `slot` instead of `document_source_id`, so a plugin issues from whatever the workspace bound; a slot whose plugin is off, that nothing fills, or whose template no longer fits is a 409 that says which (WHIT-742).
- **Templates for workspace plugins' records.** A workspace plugin's record types are document record types in the workspaces where it is on, such as `CakeOrders.order`, so a template can be written for a cake order and issued for one (`record: "CakeOrders.order:12"`). Its fields are `record.<field>`, read with the record type's own read permission and printed as a reader sees them: money with its currency, dates in words, choices by their label, a person by name. Migration 178 widens `document_sources.record_type` to 160 characters to fit them (WHIT-742).
