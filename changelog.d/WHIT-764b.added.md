- **Workspace plugins can carry their own tests (plugin SDK 1.54).** A plugin's manifest can include examples its rules must keep, such as "a low deposit can't be confirmed" or "the balance is worked out". Each example creates a record, or edits or moves one, and says whether the change should be refused or what it should leave. `/api/v1/workspace-plugins/test` runs them against the real record store and leaves nothing behind. Publishing runs them first and refuses a version whose tests fail, showing what each failing example actually got (WHIT-764).
