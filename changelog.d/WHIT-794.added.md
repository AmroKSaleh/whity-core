- **MCP: `init_session` tells an AI what it can and can't do here.** It is the first always-on tool, and its description says to call it first. It returns a short briefing, in English or Arabic, built from the same filter as `tools/list` and search, so it never claims more than the caller can do. The briefing covers who the caller acts as (themselves, or an agent for a person) and the workspace, which areas they can reach with operation counts, and for agents what is Allowed or needs a person's approval (what is turned off is only counted). It also says how to work here and gives the rate limits. `initialize` and `server/discover` carry a one-line version. It replaces `get_api_me` among the always-on tools, which stays reachable through search (WHIT-794).
