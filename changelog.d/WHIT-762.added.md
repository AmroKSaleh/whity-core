- **Rules for workspace plugin records (plugin SDK 1.51).** A record type can declare rules such as "a confirmed order needs a deposit of at least 30%", and its fields can have defaults, calculated values and locks ("price can't change once confirmed"), all written in the manifest expression language. They hold on every create, edit and move. A refused write gets the rule's message in English and Arabic against the field it concerns, and a rule that can't be checked refuses the write rather than letting it through (WHIT-762).
