- An app that Whity ID marks as an agent (`whity_agent: true` on its access token) now connects to `/mcp` as an AI agent acting for the person who signed in. It reads directly, and its changes become proposals the person approves. Other apps that sign in through Whity ID still act as the person, as before. Agents can check on what they asked for with the new read-only `get_proposal` tool (WHIT-825).
