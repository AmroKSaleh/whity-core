- **One issuing service for requests, the queue worker and the event relay.** `TemplateIssuing` issues a document from a slot or a template, for a record, with merge values, and refuses with a stable reason (`slot_unavailable`, `slot_unbound`, `slot_misfit`, `template_not_found`, `template_unpublished`, `record_not_found`, `missing_fields`, `field_conflict`, `vetoed`, `origin_key_conflict` and others) without writing anything. `POST /api/documents` now goes through it, and both `public/index.php` and the CLI kernel register it, with the slot registry and workspace plugins' record types, so automations and plugin jobs in `queue:work` and `events:relay` issue the same way a request does. The worker builds the render, storage and QR parts on its first issue (WHIT-742).
