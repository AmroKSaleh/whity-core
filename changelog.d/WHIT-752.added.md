- **Plugin SDK 1.52: MCP tool toolsets, visibility, agent policy and undo.** Plugin tool descriptors can now name a sub-toolset of their plugin's own, ask to be the plugin's one read-only always-on tool, cap what agents may do (`allow`, `propose`, `suggest-only`, `refuse`), and say how they are undone. The new `PluginMcpToolsetsInterface` gives a plugin's toolsets an English and Arabic title and a one-line description. Names must fit `[A-Za-z0-9_-]{1,64}` and descriptions 500 characters, and unprefixed names are deprecated. See `docs/wiki/Plugin-Development.md` (WHIT-752).
