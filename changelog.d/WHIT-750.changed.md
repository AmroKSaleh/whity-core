- **MCP: `/mcp` now lists a handful of everyday tools plus `search_operations` and `call_operation`, instead of every operation.** Your client finds and calls everything it could before: `search_operations` searches by what an operation does (in English or by area in Arabic), returns one operation in full when given its `operationId`, and `call_operation` calls it. To keep the old full list, use `/mcp?tools=full`. The previous discovery tools `search_tools`, `describe_tool` and `call_tool` still answer, unlisted, for one more minor release, then are removed. Calling an operation by its own name with `tools/call` keeps working (WHIT-750).
