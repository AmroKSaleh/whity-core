- **A workspace can have its own plugins, published without a deploy.** A `plugin.json` manifest can now belong to one workspace. It is stored as an immutable version and served from the next request in every worker, with no reload. Its screens appear in that workspace's features list and menu, through the same permission filter as any other screen. Its permissions become grantable and its suggested roles are created in that workspace only. Other workspaces never see its screens or its permission names. A workspace plugin can't declare tables or limits, take a name an installed plugin uses, declare permissions outside its own name, inherit an administrator's role, or point a screen at anything but its own record routes. See `docs/wiki/Workspace-Plugins.md`. This is the per-workspace runtime under Whity Builder (WHIT-760).
