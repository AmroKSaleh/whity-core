- **Issuing has its own permission, `documents:issue`.** It gates creating a document, correcting it, and minting or withdrawing its QR code. `documents:render` now means a PDF preview and label printing, which store nothing. Migration 166 grants `documents:issue` to every role that holds `documents:render`, so nobody loses access on upgrade. For agents, issuing is always a proposal (WHIT-730).
