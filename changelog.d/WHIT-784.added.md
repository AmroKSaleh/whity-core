- **Agent proposals: "Ask first" is real on the server.** When an agent's call needs a permission its grant sets to Ask first, the call doesn't run. It is stored as a proposal (operation, arguments, the permissions it uses) for the person the agent acts for, who approves, edits or declines it at `/api/me/agent-proposals`. Approving runs it once, as that person's delegate, after every check runs again. Proposals expire after 7 days, and only that person can decide. These routes are never on the MCP surface, so an agent can't approve its own proposal (migration 176; WHIT-784).
