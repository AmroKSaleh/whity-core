- **Workspace plugins can keep records (plugin SDK 1.50).** A workspace plugin's `plugin.json` can declare record types: typed fields (text, numbers, money in minor units, dates, choices, and links to people, units or other records), an optional lifecycle of states and named moves, and read, create, edit and delete permissions. The records live in one shared, workspace-scoped table, so changing a record type needs no migration. They are served at `/api/v1/x/{plugin}/{type}`, which checks every write against the declaration and every action against its declared permission. Another workspace's records answer 404. See `docs/wiki/Workspace-Plugins.md` (WHIT-761).
