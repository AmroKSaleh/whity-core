- **Documents are issued from the document editor's templates, and the old Document designer is gone.** Upgrading converts every workspace's old templates into editor drafts: a canvas in reading order, saved blocks placed where they were used, and placeholders as the template's own fields. Documents issued from old templates keep their PDFs, codes and routes but can't be re-rendered; their record page links the converted template. `POST /api/documents` now takes `document_source_id`, `record` and `values` (WHIT-730).
- **New document opens the editor.** Choose a published template and it opens previewed with the record, with the template's own fields beside it and Issue as the main action. Edit before issuing makes your own copy to change first. Document templates lists the editor's templates and label sheets, with Whity's starters to use a copy of (WHIT-730).
