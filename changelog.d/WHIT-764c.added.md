- **Workspace plugin routes are in the API reference and reachable over MCP.** Listing, validating, planning, testing, publishing and rolling back a workspace's own plugins, and reading its automation runs, are now documented in `openapi.json` and both typed clients. They are also MCP tools in the Plugins toolset. Validating, planning and testing are marked read-only for agents. Publishing, rolling back and switching automations are always proposals that a person approves (WHIT-764).
